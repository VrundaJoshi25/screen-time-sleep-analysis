# Google Form specification — copy-paste ready (Act 2)

**Settings:** collect emails **OFF** · limit to 1 response **OFF** (anonymous) ·
link responses to a Google Sheet · estimated time ≤ 3 minutes.

**Strata (quota 25 each, stop when all 4 are full):**
male-junior, male-senior, female-junior, female-senior
(Junior = 1st/2nd year; Senior = 3rd year and above, incl. PG)

---

**Section 1 — Consent**

1. **Consent** *(required, multiple choice)*
   "This is an anonymous, voluntary survey for a student research project on
   screen time and sleep. No names or emails are collected. Do you agree to
   participate?" — Options: Yes, I agree / No (No → skip to end)

**Section 2 — About you (stratification)**

2. **Gender** *(required, multiple choice)*: Male / Female / Prefer not to say
3. **Program and year of study** *(required, multiple choice)*:
   B.Tech 1st year / B.Tech 2nd year / B.Tech 3rd year / B.Tech 4th year /
   M.Sc / M.Tech / PhD / Other
4. **Age** *(required, short answer, validation: number between 15 and 60)*

**Section 3 — Screen time (from your phone's log, not memory)**

5. **Daily average screen time** *(required, short answer, validation: number 0–20)*
   "Open your phone: Settings → Screen Time (iPhone) or Digital Wellbeing
   (Android). Look at the **daily average for the past week**. What number of
   **hours** does it show? (e.g., 6.5)"
6. **Recreational share** *(required, short answer, validation: number 0–20)*
   "Of that daily average, roughly how many **hours** were recreational —
   social media, videos, games?"

**Section 4 — Sleep**

7. **Sleep duration** *(required, short answer, validation: number 0–14)*
   "Over the past week, on average, how many **hours** did you sleep per night?"
8. **Sleep quality** *(required, linear scale 1–5)*: 1 = very poor, 5 = very good
9. **Pre-bed screen use** *(required, Yes/No)*
   "Do you use screens in the 30 minutes before sleeping on most nights?"

**Section 5 — Confounders**

10. **Academic workload** *(required, short answer, validation: number 0–16)*
    "How many hours per day do you spend on academic work (classes + study + assignments)?"
11. **Caffeine** *(required, short answer, validation: number 0–15)*
    "How many servings of caffeine (coffee / tea / energy drink / cola) per day?"
12. **Exercise** *(required, short answer, validation: number 0–7)*
    "How many days per week do you exercise (≥ 30 min)?"

---

**Before distributing:** pilot on 3 friends — time them, ask what was
confusing, fix wording, THEN distribute per stratum (class/hostel groups).
Track a simple 2×2 quota grid; push wherever a cell lags.
